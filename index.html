How to read the data

Reference model Standard FMJ, buckshot and bow values transcribed from the wardo.gs ballistics table for the stated conditions. They are modeled game-file values, not experimentally confirmed live kills.

Experimental AP, HP and slug figures use published projectile/armor parameters and an independent older firing-range model. They are not verified on Season 2 build CL-516586. In particular, slug pellet-independent behavior is easier to estimate than buckshot's real hit probability.

Unavailable Missing rate-of-fire or ammunition data, or a reload required before the killing shot.

Methodology & sources

  • Target: 100 HP, 50 m, upper torso. No attachments. Zero misses.
  • Base weapon prices, damage, caliber and RPM: wardo.gs weapons, extracted October 16, 2026 (CL-516586).
  • Source-modeled baseline TTK/STK: wardo.gs ballistics.
  • Armor reductions and damage zones: wardo.gs damage model.
  • Experimental AP/HP estimates informed by WARDOGS LAB (Sept 2026). For current comparisons, cross-check wardogs.tools.
  • Source models count first-bullet flight time as part of TTK. This differs from the usual competitive-game convention of TTK starting at first impact.
